Use it

        
Open your app (for example http://localhost:3000), then click the toolbar icon or press Alt+Shift+E.

        
    
          
  • Click an element to select it. ↑ / ↓ moves to the parent or child, Shift+click selects several.
    • 
          
  • Enter the project folder that holds the site's code, type what to change, and press Send.
    • 
          
  • Every change can be undone from the panel.
    • 
          
  • To fix the phone layout, open DevTools' device toolbar (⌘⇧M / Ctrl+Shift+M) before picking.
